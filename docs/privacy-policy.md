# DocForge 导航侧栏 隐私政策 / Privacy Policy

最后更新：2026-11-27

## 中文

DocForge 导航侧栏（以下简称“本扩展”）用于把浏览器中的 JSON、JSONC、YAML、Markdown 纯文本页面与 .docx 文档渲染为可读视图，并在侧栏生成与正文联动的结构导航。

**我们不收集、不上传任何用户数据。** 具体说明：

1. 页面与文档内容：仅在您的设备本地读取与解析，用于生成渲染视图和结构树。内容不会发送到我们的服务器或任何第三方服务器，也不会写入持久存储。
2. 本地文件：仅当您主动选择文件时读取；预览/编辑期间内容临时存放在浏览器会话存储中，关闭浏览器即清空。另存副本由您自行发起，不会覆盖原文件。
3. 唯一的外部网络请求：当您打开以 .docx 结尾的网页直链时，本扩展会携带该网站的 Cookie 向该文档的原地址取回文档字节，以便本地渲染。请求只发往您正在访问的站点，不经过我们的服务器。
4. 设置与闹钟：界面语言偏好、您自建的定时闹钟配置保存在浏览器本地存储中，仅保存在您的设备上，不涉及云端同步。
5. 我们不使用分析、统计、广告或崩溃上报组件，不读取浏览历史、剪贴板、位置、通讯录、健康或财务信息，不出售、出租或向任何第三方共享用户数据。
6. 本扩展不使用远程代码：所有代码随扩展包下发，不使用 eval 或远程脚本加载。
7. 权限用途：sidePanel 用于展示侧栏导航；tabs 用于与正文所在标签页收发导航消息；storage 用于保存设置与闹钟；alarms 与 notifications 用于在您设定的时间提醒；declarativeNetRequest 仅用于把 .docx 直链重定向到扩展内置阅读页；主机权限仅用于在上述文档型页面注入只读渲染与导航。

如本政策有变更，会更新本页面的“最后更新”日期。联系方式：请通过加载项商店详情页所列的支持邮箱与我们联系。

## English

DocForge Sidebar (the “extension”) renders plain-text JSON, JSONC, YAML and Markdown pages, and .docx documents, into a readable view, and builds a linked outline tree in the sidebar.

**We do not collect or transmit any user data.**

1. Page and document content is read and parsed only on your device to render the view and outline. It is never sent to our servers or any third party, and never written to persistent storage.
2. Local files are read only when you pick them; content is held in browser session storage while previewing or editing and is cleared when the browser closes. Saving a copy is initiated by you and never overwrites the original file.
3. The only external request occurs when you open a web link ending in .docx: the extension fetches that document from its original address with your cookies for that site, so it can be rendered locally. The request goes only to the site you are visiting, never to our servers.
4. UI language preference and the alarms you create are stored in local browser storage on your device only.
5. No analytics, tracking, advertising or crash-reporting components are used. We do not access browsing history, clipboard, location, contacts, health or financial data, and we never sell, rent or share user data with third parties.
6. The extension uses no remote code: all code ships inside the package, with no eval or remotely loaded scripts.
7. Permission use: sidePanel for the sidebar outline; tabs to exchange navigation messages with the document tab; storage for settings and alarms; alarms and notifications to remind you at the times you set; declarativeNetRequest solely to redirect .docx links to the built-in reader; host permissions solely to inject the read-only renderer on such document pages.
